Architecture

Direct where it matters.

Verge separates rendezvous from the actual conversation. Signaling helps peers find each other; WebRTC carries media and data. Production rooms authenticate entry and recover bounded signaling interruptions without moving conference media into the control plane.

01 · CAPTURE

Your browser owns your devices

Camera, microphone, display capture, device selection, and media processing stay at the client boundary.

camerapeer
02 · RENDEZVOUS

Control stays small

The control plane authenticates room entry, issues time-limited TURN credentials, tracks ephemeral membership, and forwards SDP/ICE messages.

SDP/ICEroom
03 · TRANSPORT

Peers carry the room

SRTP handles media and ordered data channels carry chat and files. A topology-neutral contract leaves room for a future SFU.
